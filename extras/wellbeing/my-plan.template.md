# My plan

Fill this in on a calm week, in your own words. Any section can stay empty. You can also
ask Claude to go through it with you ("let's fill in my plan"); it will ask one section
at a time and write down what you say, not its version of it.

## How I know I'm going up

The first signs, the ones I or people close to me notice before I do.
(For example: sleeping five hours and feeling fine, starting several projects at once,
talking faster, spending, messaging lots of people, everything feeling urgent.)
